About Daily Bread

A year through the whole Bible

Daily Bread is a Messianic one-year Bible reading plan. It follows the weekly Torah portion and the biblical Hebrew calendar, with its Sabbaths, feasts and new moons, so that reading the Scriptures keeps time with the rhythm God gave to Israel.

Each weekday has four readings: one from the Torah, one from the Prophets, one from the Writings and one from the Apostles. The Torah readings move through the portion of the week, and on Shabbat the portion is read with its Haftarah from the Prophets and a reading from the Apostles. On new moons and feast days the special readings for the day are added. The aim is simple: to help disciples of the Messiah become disciplined students of the whole Bible.

The reading plan was prepared by Lars Enarson and is published by Ariel Media, the publishing ministry of The Watchman International. The present year, 5787, runs from 4 October 2026 to 24 October 2027.

Reading here

Daily Bread Online is free. Each day’s readings are given in the Berean Standard Bible, with the Hebrew and Greek text, word study for every Hebrew and Greek word, maps for key passages, the Jewish calendar and Shabbat times.

At the end of each day’s reading you can mark it as read. The calendar then shows the days you have read, and if you miss a few days the site offers to help you catch up, or simply to pick up again from today. Your progress is kept in this browser on this device only. Nothing is sent to us, and clearing your browser data erases it.

The printed journal

The Daily Bread journal follows the same plan in print: a page for each day with room to write what you learn, the Hebrew calendar with its feasts and historical notes, the blessings for the study of Torah and a list of all the Torah portions. It is a hardcover book, in Classic and Women’s editions, with Daily Bread for Kids for younger readers.
