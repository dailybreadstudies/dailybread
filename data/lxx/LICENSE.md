# Septuagint (Greek Old Testament)

**Text:** Henry Barclay Swete, *The Old Testament in Greek according to the Septuagint* (Cambridge University
Press; Vol. I 4th ed. 1909, Vol. II 3rd ed. 1907, Vol. III 4th ed. 1912). The printed edition is in the public domain
(scans: archive.org, *theoldtestamenti00unknuoft*, *oldtestamenting02swet*, *oldtestgreek00unknuoft*).

**Digital edition:** supplied by Pasquale Amicarelli (formerly a BibleWorks module); published by Eliran Wong at
https://github.com/eliranwong/LXX-Swete-1930 under the **GNU General Public License v3.0** (full text in
`LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt`). The files in this folder are a modified version of that data and are released under the
same licence.

**Changes made:** the BibleWorks marks ⸂ ⸃ ⸆ and Swete's sub-verse numbers ([1], [2] …) were removed from the text;
verses were regrouped under the Berean Standard Bible's chapter and verse numbers, with the Septuagint's own number
kept beside each verse where it differs, verses absent from Swete's text listed, and Septuagint-only passages kept
separately. Built by `tools/build_lxx.py`; hand corrections in `tools/lxx-overrides.json`.

**Versification:** STEPBible TVTMS — Translators Versification Traditions with Methodology for Standardisation,
Tyndale House Cambridge / STEPBible.org, CC BY 4.0, https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data.
