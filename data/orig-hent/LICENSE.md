# Hebrew New Testament (Delitzsch)

`data/orig-hent/*.json` — the Hebrew New Testament translated by Franz Delitzsch (8th edition, 1885), as transcribed
in https://github.com/HebrewNewTestament/HebDelitzsch (`base.osis`), with the vowel pointing edited by
Streams in the Negev (2003).

- Delitzsch's translation is in the public domain.
- The vowel pointing is © 2003 Streams in the Negev and is "free for use by any non-commercial project"
  (STEPBible / CrossWire module notes for HebDelitzsch). Daily Bread Online is free and non-commercial.

Built with `python3 tools/build_hebnt.py <HebDelitzsch/base.osis> data/orig-hent`.
Verse numbers follow the BSB (2 Corinthians 13:12–14 re-divided accordingly).
