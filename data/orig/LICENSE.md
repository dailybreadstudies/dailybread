# Original-language texts

These files are arranged by the Berean Standard Bible's chapter and verse numbers for use by this site.

**Hebrew (Genesis–Malachi):** *Miqra according to the Masorah* (MAM), prepared by Seth (Avi) Kadish
from materials developed at Hebrew Wikisource, with technical assistance from Erel Segal-Halevi and
Benjamin Denckla. Source: the MAM-for-Sefaria edition in https://github.com/bdenckla/MAM-basics.
Attribution: [Hebrew Wikisource — Miqra according to the Masorah](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/User:Dovi/Miqra_according_to_the_Masorah#beginning).
License: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). These Hebrew files are a
derivative (verse numbers aligned to English versification; markup simplified) and are shared under the same license.

**Greek (Matthew–Revelation):** *SBL Greek New Testament*, copyright 2010 Society of Biblical Literature
and Logos Bible Software. Source: https://github.com/LogosBible/SBLGNT.
License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Includes the SBLGNT apparatus notes
(`n` in each Greek file) and its markers (⸀ ⸁ ⸂ ⸃ ⸄ ⸅); verse numbers aligned to the BSB.

Rebuild with `tools/build_originals.py`.
