Guide

How to use Daily Bread Online.

A short tour of the reader: mark your readings and watch your Bible fill in, read the Hebrew and Greek, and open the maps beside the text.

Your progress Original languages Bible maps

1Your progress

Mark each reading, watch your Bible fill in.

Every day has four readings: Torah, Prophets, Writings and Apostles. When you finish one, mark it complete, and the Progress page fills in its verses, book by book.

  1. Tap the circle beside a reading at the top of the day, or the check at the end of the text.
  2. Open Progress to see the whole Bible as a circle, and every book as a bar that fills in as you read.
  3. Missed a day? It waits under To catch up, with one tap to mark the whole day read.

Your progress is kept in this browser. Sign in to keep it on all your devices.

Try it today See your progress

2Original languages

The Hebrew and Greek, under every verse.

Read the Scriptures in the languages they were written in, right beside the English.

  1. Open the A / א menu at the top right of the reader.
  2. Under Original Languages, choose Under Verses for the Hebrew or Greek beneath each verse, or Only for the original text on its own.
  3. For the Apostles, choose the Greek, the Hebrew New Testament, or both.
  4. Tap any Hebrew or Greek word for its meaning and form.
Try it today

3Bible maps

Maps beside the verses they bring to life.

Where a passage moves through places, a map button sits in the margin beside the verse.

  1. Tap the map button beside the verse. The map opens next to the text, or just below the verse on a phone.
  2. Tap a name in the key to show or hide it on the map.
  3. Switch between Ancient, Modern and Satellite, or Enlarge the map to fill the screen.
Try it in Genesis 10
