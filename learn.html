Messiah in the Torah

Weekly Bible commentaries by Lars Enarson

Torah does not mean “law” in the modern sense. It means “instruction” and “teaching.” In every synagogue around the world a portion of the Torah is read each Sabbath, and the apostles encouraged disciples who were not Jewish to follow these readings (Acts 15:21). Every Christian needs to study the Torah to understand the message of the Bible and who Jesus really is.

For twelve years Bible teacher Lars Enarson worked on a commentary on the weekly Torah portion. Many commentaries are filled with an abundance of knowledge; the goal of this one is discipleship to Jesus. It focuses on two things: the centrality of Messiah, and practical discipleship to him in everything.
