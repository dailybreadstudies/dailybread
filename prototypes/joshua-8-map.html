Where was Ai?

The map places Ai at et-Tell, the identification most scholars follow and OpenBible.info's top choice. Its Arabic name means "the mound", which echoes Joshua 8:28: Joshua made Ai a tel olam, a heap forever. The excavations at et-Tell found no city there in the period most often given for Joshua, so some scholars, notably Bryant Wood, argue for Khirbet el-Maqatir, about one kilometre to the west.

About the routes

The text gives the positions: the ambush between Bethel and Ai, west of the city (vv. 9, 12); the main camp north of Ai with a valley between (v. 11); the retreat toward the wilderness (v. 15). The lines that connect them are reconstructions drawn for teaching, not paths the text describes. Dashed lines mark routes the text does not mention at all.

Verse 3 sends out 30,000 men and verse 12 speaks of about 5,000 in ambush. Commentators explain the difference in several ways, so the map labels each figure with its verse.
